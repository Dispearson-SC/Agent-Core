## 4. Is there a Jev / TypeSafe MLH prize?

### Takeaway
No evidence found. TypeSafe launched Jev on 15 Sep 2026. No MLH, Devpost or TypeSafe source found links Jev to an MLH prize category.
Treat "Best Use of TypeSafe/Jev" as **UNCONFIRMED and probably absent**.

### Cited Findings
- TypeSafe came out of stealth on 15 Sep 2026 with $40M seed funding. Jev is a "System One" model that returns typed probabilistic decisions, not text. Vendor-quoted figures: about $0.042 per million input tokens, free output, 70–500 ms latency. It is available through Cloudflare Workers AI and an OpenRouter beta — [TypeSafe blog](https://typesafe.ai/blog/introducing-system-one-models-and-jev), [DataCamp](https://www.datacamp.com/blog/system-one-models-jev), [OpenRouter](https://openrouter.ai/%7Etypesafe/jev-latest)
- Jev cannot write, summarise or explain its choice — [search summary of llmreference / kanerika](https://kanerika.com/blogs/jev-vs-llm/)

### Inferences
- Jev's typed action-plus-confidence output fits bycode's "recommend action plus confidence" closely. Its inability to explain means a second LLM (for example Gemini) is needed for the "justify" requirement, which is also a natural way to stack prizes.
- If Jev is reached via Cloudflare Workers AI, it could count toward a Cloudflare category, but no 2027 Cloudflare MLH prize was found.

### Gaps
- TypeSafe's own social accounts and the Saltillo page were not checked; both were blocked.

### Takeaway
It is road2tech.com's own challenge-registration counter. **Each challenge admits at most 12 teams, and the choice is
final** (CONFIRMED). This means one local sponsor challenge per team. MLH categories are separate and can be stacked.

### Cited Findings
- "Each challenge admits a maximum of 12 teams, and the choice is final" — [Road To Tech 2026](https://www.road2tech.com/)
- Total capacity is 45 teams — [Road To Tech evento](https://www.road2tech.com/evento)

### Inferences
- With 45 teams and a cap of 12 per challenge, there are probably about 4 local challenges.
- Stacking tips (inferred): pick bycode as the one local challenge. Then design one project that legitimately uses:
  - Backboard for per-entity memory and threads (Best Use of Backboard)
  - Gemini API for the justification text or as the reasoning model (Best Use of Gemini API)
  - ElevenLabs for a voice alert of the recommended action (Best Use of ElevenLabs)
  - a registered GoDaddy Registry domain for the demo (domain challenge)

  Each integration must be real and visible in the demo and the write-up. Do not bolt on Solana unless a natural use exists.
